# DailyOS — PR / Press Kit

Free press is the fastest way to a spike of thousands of visitors with no ad
spend. UK consumer/money desks love a "you're wasting money without realising"
story — DailyOS fits it perfectly. Below: the angle, the pitch email, target
outlets, and the assets a journalist will ask for.

---

## The angle (this is what gets picked up)

Journalists don't care about your app. They care about a story their readers
will click. Lead with the **problem + a number**, not the product:

- **"The average UK household wastes £X a year on subscriptions they've
  forgotten about."** (Use a credible published stat — e.g. Citizens Advice /
  Barclays / finder.com have all published figures on unused subscriptions;
  cite the source. Do NOT invent numbers.)
- **"Free-trial traps and auto-renewals cost Brits £Xbn a year."**
- **The human angle:** life admin as invisible labour / mental load — strong for
  parenting and women's-interest desks, and for ADHD/mental-health angles.

DailyOS is then the *solution* in the story, not the subject.

## Pitch email (short — journalists skim)

> **Subject:** Story idea: the £X Brits waste on forgotten subscriptions (+ a free tool)
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Loved your recent piece on [specific article — always personalise]. Quick idea
> that might suit [outlet]'s readers.
>
> [Credible stat with source] — most people underestimate their subscriptions by
> [x]. I've built a free, no-sign-up tool that lets anyone add theirs and see the
> real yearly total in seconds: [link]. It consistently surprises people.
>
> It's the free tool from DailyOS, an app I've built that reads your receipts,
> letters and emails and turns them into tasks, reminders and a tidy record — so
> renewals and trials stop slipping through. Happy to give you:
> • the free tool to link for readers,
> • a short data breakdown / quotes,
> • free access to try the app.
>
> Would a "how to audit your subscriptions in 10 minutes" angle be useful? Happy
> to write a tight version for you.
>
> Thanks,
> [Your name] — founder, DailyOS
> [phone] · [link]

**Rules:** one outlet at a time, personalised; follow up once after 3–4 days;
mornings are best; never attach — link.

## Target outlets (UK consumer / money / lifestyle)

- **Money-saving:** MoneySavingExpert (forum + news tips), Be Clever With Your
  Cash, The Money Edit, lovemoney, finder.com, NimbleFins.
- **Nationals (money/consumer desks):** The Sun (Money), Metro, Mirror Money,
  Express Money, The i, Guardian Money, Telegraph Money.
- **Lifestyle / parenting / women's:** Stylist, Grazia, Mumsnet (Bloggers
  Network / guest posts), Netmums.
- **Tech/startup:** UKTN, TechRound, Startups.co.uk, Tech.eu (better for a
  funding/milestone angle than a consumer one).
- **Local:** your regional paper + BBC local radio love a local-founder story —
  easy, underrated wins.

## Also do

- **HARO / #JournoRequest** (X) and **ResponseSource / Featured.com** — reply to
  journalists already writing about money/productivity/subscriptions.
- **Podcasts** — personal finance and productivity pods take founder guests;
  pitch the same problem-first angle.

## Assets to have ready (journalists will ask)

- 1–2 line boilerplate about DailyOS (see LAUNCH_KIT positioning).
- Founder name, headshot, one quote.
- The free tool link (their reader hook).
- 2–3 screenshots + the 30s demo clip.
- Any real usage numbers once you have them ("X people have checked their
  subscriptions with our tool").

---

## The one rule

Never fabricate a statistic or a user number. Cite real published sources for the
"waste" figure, and only claim usage numbers you actually have. A single made-up
stat that a journalist catches ends the relationship — and the story.
